# VipervisionAI — AI Tool Matchmaker

Find the right AI tool for your small business, in minutes.

### About the Project:

Viper Vision AI was developed by our team for the University of Maryland AI for Small Business Hackathon.

The application includes Vee, a conversational AI assistant that helps small-business owners identify problems and find relevant AI tools.

Vee collects information about the business, including its challenges, budget, employee count, and technology preferences. This information is used to evaluate products and subscription plans from our researched catalog.

### How It Works:


Business intake: Vee gathers information through a conversation with the user.

Tool matching: The application compares the user's requirements against documented product capabilities, pricing, and limitations.

Recommendations: Plans are classified as Strong Fit, Partial Fit, or Research Lead based on available information.

Next steps: Vee explains the results and can suggest practical ways to address the business problem.


###Features:

Conversational AI assistant

Searchable catalog of AI tools

Subscription-plan comparisons

Budget and employee-count considerations

Source references and research dates

ROI calculator

Implementation guidance



### The catalog is stored in two CSV files:

ai_tools.csv — Product information, descriptions, and research sources.

tool_plans.csv — Subscription plans, pricing, supported capabilities, and limitations.

Product information is researched using vendor websites and documentation. Pricing and features may change, and not every product has enough available information to qualify as a verified recommendation.
